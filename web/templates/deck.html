Where this is going

The ranking above exists because a deck's score means nothing on its own. "0.24" is not a sentence. "More original than every precon but two" is one, and saying it needs a fair population to measure against: same size, same format, same brief. That is what the precons are for, and it is the piece that had to come first.

What it makes possible is the same reading applied to a list you paste in, which is the part being built now.
