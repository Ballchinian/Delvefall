What the figures on each card mean
Originality
One minus how close the card's nearest match anywhere in Magic gets, so a card at 0.30 has something out there 70% like it.
Play rate
EDHREC's rank for how often the card is played in Commander, where #1 is the most played card in the format.
Salt
Out of about 3, from EDHREC's salt survey, where players vote on the cards they least enjoy facing.
Card age
How long ago the card was first printed, counted from its earliest printing, so a reprint does not make an old card new.
