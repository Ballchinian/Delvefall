How this is measured, and what it doesn't measure

Every card carries an originality score: one minus how close its closest match anywhere else in Magic gets. A deck's score is the average of its {{ top_n }} most original nonland cards. Averaging all 100 instead just measures the mana base and the removal package, which every deck shares.

It is not measuring how good or how expensive a deck is. Against the decks' median EDHREC rank the correlation is r = -0.05, which is nothing. Original and popular turn out to be unrelated.

It is partly measuring how recent a deck is (r = +0.46 against release year). Some of that is real: design space fills up, so a card from 2011 has had fifteen years of imitators, and being imitated is the opposite of being unique. The ranking still separates decks inside a single era, which is what the era buttons are for.
